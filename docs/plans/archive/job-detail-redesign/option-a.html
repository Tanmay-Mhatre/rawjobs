What changed and why

  1. Verdict in the header. Score plus one sentence ("Strong match … salary not listed") sits under the title, so the pane answers "is this worth my time?" before any scrolling.
  2. One freshness phrase. "Posted 2h ago · live on Ashby 6m ago" replaces the fresh line, the Details table rows and the repeat inside Why it matched.
  3. Facts as chips, unknowns as one line. The six-box Key facts grid is gone. Known facts become chips; anything missing collapses into "Not stated: salary".
  4. Details table dropped. Department and Industry are chips under the title, Hiring system lives in the freshness phrase and the Apply tooltip.
  5. Company signal next to the name. "12 roles in 30 days" merges Company hiring and Hiring activity into one place.
  6. LinkedIn as a one-time banner. A dismissible ask at the top with the privacy promise up front, instead of a paragraph mid-pane. Once connected it turns into a people block and a "3 you know" chip.
  7. Everything else is a quiet row. Why it matched, About the role, Notes and More at SplitMetrics fold into rows with a count on the right; open on demand.
  8. Empty states collapse. No description becomes one row with "Open job page"; sections with nothing in them don't render, so a thin job is a short pane.
  9. Clear footer. Apply is primary; Save is labelled (and becomes a status pill once tracked). Not interested and Hide company move into the ⋯ menu, so no X sits beside Apply.
  10. Same product. Sand/orange tokens, Geist, 2–4px radius, 4/8px spacing, borders before shadows; dark mode flips the same tokens.
