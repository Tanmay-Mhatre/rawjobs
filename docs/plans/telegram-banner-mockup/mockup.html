How it behaves

  • Shows when: Telegram isn't connected, RawJobs runs on this computer, and the first scan has finished. Never during setup: the checklist row already covers that.
  • On phones (under 640 px): starts folded: headline, two lines of text, Read more, then the buttons on one row. About a third of the screen, so jobs stay in view. Read more shows the full text, the lock line and the reminders tip; Show less folds it again. Wider screens always show it in full.
  • Connect Telegram: opens the same two-step dialog as the scan bell (BotFather token, then say hi). The user stays on the Radar.
  • Extra line: only when daily scans and the daily summary are both off. Its links open Settings → Scans and Settings → Notifications.
  • Not now: hides it for 7 days. The second "Not now" switches to the quiet line (5). Its × hides it for good. Settings → Notifications still works any time.
  • After connecting: the success line (4), then nothing. The bot sends the updated first message (6).
  • Disconnecting later doesn't bring the banner back. The quiet line (5) does, unless it was closed with ×.
  • Copy facts: new-job messages come from scheduled scans and the background check. "Scan finished" messages come from scans you start. Update messages need the installed app. Reminders only go out with scheduled scans or the daily summary.
