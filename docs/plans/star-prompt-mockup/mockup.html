Checklist, tips and the "Star on GitHub" question

When it shows

7+ days since first useand1+ job marked Applied or 5+ finished scansandon Radar, no scan running, no other popup→show once
  • Star on GitHub: opens github.com/Tanmay-Mhatre/rawjobs in a new tab. Never asks again.
  • Remind me later (or Esc / backdrop): asks once more after 30 days, then never.
  • Don't ask again: never asks again. "Show them again" (Settings → Data & privacy → Tips you've closed) resets it.

State lives in rawjobs.starPrompt = { firstSeen, later[], answer }, added to KEPT_ON_DISK. For existing users, firstSeen is backfilled from the earliest scan in meta.json or tracking change still on file. The popup joins ServerNotices last, after the required update, What's new and the shortcut intro. Counts in the chips come from real data. Chips with a 0 are hidden. Nothing is sent anywhere.
