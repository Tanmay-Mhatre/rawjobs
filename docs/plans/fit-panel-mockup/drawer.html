What changed, section by section

  1. One section style. Every block gets the same small uppercase heading, an optional count, and its action on the right, divided by one hairline. Before, each section had its own look, so the page read as one long scroll.
  2. Order follows the decision: fit → the facts → who can get you in → the role itself → your progress → the company. Things you act on sit above things you read.
  3. Fit starts folded (verdict + what's wrong). "Why" opens the score rows from the fit mockup.
  4. Key facts become a label/value grid instead of a pill cloud. "Not stated" values show in grey in place, not in a separate line.
  5. People you know: top 3 as real rows (initials, role, "likely to help" tag, one Message button). "Show all", the LinkedIn search and "Find friends" fold into one line of links.
  6. LinkedIn import ask stays a callout above the fold but shorter; closing it moves the ask into the People section.
  7. About the role: your topics highlighted, faded preview, "Read full". In Wide (F) it takes the left column.
  8. Your progress: status, CV, follow-up date as one label/value block; Activity / People / Interviews tabs below.
  9. Company merges three sections: hiring pace (one line, no 12-month bar chart), other locations of this role, and more jobs there.
