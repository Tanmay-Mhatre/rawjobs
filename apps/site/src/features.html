Features

Everything it does, in one place

01

Find jobs straight from the source

It reads companies' own careers pages, not reposts, so when you apply, your resume lands in their hiring system.

  • A shared directory of __COMPANIES_ROUND__ companies

    Which hiring system and board each company uses, whether it's live, and its industries. Rebuilt every week from public sources, with no AI, and downloaded by your copy when yours is more than a week old.

  • __SYSTEMS_N__ hiring systems, __BOARDS_N__ job boards

    Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters and many more, plus public boards such as Hacker News "Who is hiring", Remotive and We Work Remotely.

  • Two scan scopes

    • My companies + my industries: your companies plus every live company tagged with your industries. A minute or two.
    • All companies: every live board in the directory. Longer, and you can stop and resume it.

  • Add any company by link

    Paste a careers page and RawJobs works out its hiring system. Or browse the directory and get suggestions that fit your profile.

  • Fast scans that skip the empty ones

    A shared job feed tells each scan which companies have nothing passing your title and place rules, so it can skip them. Everything else is fetched live by your own scan: every job you see came from the company's page.

02

A score you can read

No AI and no black box. Every job gets 0 to 100 from rules you set, and the reason is written out on every job.

  • Four parts, out of 100

    Up to 30 for the title (and how close its level is to yours), 20 for the place, 40 for your topics in the posting, and 10 for the company's industry.

    Title 30Place 20Topics 27/40Industry 10= 87
    Example

  • Gates first

    The title must have one of your titles and none you excluded, and the place must be yours or an accepted remote region. Anything else scores 0 and is hidden by default.

  • Dealbreakers

    • Salary floor: the posted range tops out more than 5% under it
    • Work authorization: it won't sponsor where you'd need it
    • Avoid: work you won't do
    • Residents-only postings for another country

    The job still shows, with the reason, but it's never a strong match.

  • Fresh, but fair

    Best match adds a freshness boost that halves every 3 days, so a strong job posted today beats an equal one from last week, and a weak new job never leaps a strong one.

  • Your rules, your threshold

    Choose what counts as a strong match. Change titles, places, topics and dealbreakers any time in Settings, or edit the config file by hand.

Track every application

From saved to offer, and which companies are still worth watching.

  • Company health

05

New matches with your coffee

Set a time, and RawJobs scans and messages you. Nothing to remember.

  • Scheduled scans

    Pick one or two times a day and a scope. It uses your computer's own scheduler (Task Scheduler, launchd or a systemd timer), runs while the computer is on, and catches up on a missed run.

  • Telegram alerts

    A 2-minute guided setup with your own bot, then a test message. Each scheduled scan sends one digest of new matches, and nothing when nothing is new.

    RawJobs: new matches92 · Head of Product, Exchange · Acme Exchange81 · Senior PM, Payments · Halcyon Labs
    Example message

  • A command line, too

    Everything the dashboard does, from the terminal: rawjobs scan --notify, rawjobs schedule install --time 08:00, rawjobs detect <careers-url>, rawjobs companies suggest.

06

Set up in about 3 minutes

The dashboard walks you through it the first time. Skip any step and come back later.

  • Start from your resume

    Paste it and get suggested roles, places and topics. Keep several versions? A ready prompt for your own Claude or ChatGPT merges them into one master resume. It stays on your computer.

  • Roles from 33 job families

    Pick your family and its titles appear as chips. Add titles from any family with search.

  • Any place in the world

    Search any country, city or region. A country brings its main cities with it; drop single cities in a click. Choose whether remote counts, and where.

  • 50 industries in 7 groups

    Crypto, Fintech, AI and more. Companies in your industries are scanned and suggested to you.

07

On your computer

No account and no tracking. You own the app, the data and the code.

  • One-line install

    On Windows, Mac and Linux. No Node, git, account or admin rights. It starts when you log in and adds a RawJobs shortcut.

  • Updates itself, safely

    New versions download in the background. Install them automatically, ask first, or never. If a new version doesn't start, it goes back to the one that did.

  • Private by design

    Your resume, settings, statuses and run history stay on your computer, and the app sends no usage data. It only talks to job boards, GitHub and, if you set it up, Telegram.

  • Light, dark, high contrast

    Follow your system or pick a theme, each with an increased-contrast version, and choose how dense the job list is.

  • Open source, and open to help

    The code is MIT licensed and the shared directory is CC BY 4.0. Suggest a company, add a hiring system (each one is a small file), or run your own copy of the directory.
