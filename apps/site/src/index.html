The problem

Apply where the job lives

Sent 50 applications and heard back from 2? Where you applied may be part of the reason.

Many job sites repost jobs instead of hosting them.

Aggregators and agencies often repost openings from company careers pages. Copies can go stale, and some feed a talent pool rather than the hiring team. Apply there and your resume can stop with the middleman instead of reaching the team that's hiring. Most jobs in RawJobs come straight from the company's own careers page, so the apply link goes into the company's own hiring system.

Where your resume goes

Through middlemen 2 in between

Through middlemen: your resume goes to a job aggregator, then a staffing agency, and often never reaches the company. No reply.

Direct to the company nothing in between

Direct to the company: your resume goes to the company's own careers page and into its own hiring system.

Not every repost is a dead end, and applying direct doesn't guarantee a reply. It does remove the hops where resumes get lost.

Scoring

A score you can argue with

No AI and no black box. Title up to 30, place up to 20, keywords up to 40, industry up to 10. Newer jobs rank higher, separately. Change what you're looking for and watch the score move.

87title 30, place 20, keywords 27, industry 10
Titles you want

The job title must contain one of these.

Your seniority word

The title is Head level. The same level adds 10, one level away adds 5.

Places

The job must be in one of these, or remote where you accept it.

Keywords

Points are the share of your keywords the posting mentions. 3 matches fill the bar. A keyword in the title is worth up to 20.

The company's industry

Yours adds 10, not known adds 5, another one adds 0.

Get it

Two ways to run it, both on your computer

Install it with one line, or run it from the source code. Either way it runs on your computer, and nothing about you is uploaded.

Install in one line

Works today

No Node, git, account or admin rights needed. Paste one line into a terminal: it installs RawJobs in your user folder, starts it when you log in, keeps it up to date, and opens your dashboard in the browser.

  1. Windows, in PowerShell: irm https://github.com/Tanmay-Mhatre/rawjobs/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex
  2. macOS, Linux, in Terminal: curl -fsSL https://github.com/Tanmay-Mhatre/rawjobs/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

Privacy

Runs on your machine

Your resume and roles stay on your computer. If you add a company by link, only its careers board is shared, and you can turn that off. RawJobs only reads job postings that companies publish themselves.

Your profile stays local

Resume, config, run history and alert settings are files on your computer, gitignored by default.

No account, no telemetry

Nothing to sign up for. The app only talks to job boards, GitHub for the shared directory and for updates (once a day, and you can turn it off), the project's company inbox when you add a company (optional) and, if you turn on alerts, Telegram. Installing also downloads Node from nodejs.org.

Public postings only

It never touches apply forms or candidate data, and every job links back to the original posting.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. RawJobs is MIT licensed, with no paid tier and no account.

Does it use AI?

No. Scores come from keyword rules you can read and change. An optional AI re-rank, with your own key, is on the roadmap.

Where do the jobs come from?

From each company's own careers page on its hiring system, like Greenhouse, Lever or Workday, plus 4 public job boards. A shared directory of __COMPANIES_ROUND__ companies, __LIVE_ROUND__ with live job boards, rebuilt weekly, tells RawJobs where to look.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Only one thing, and you can turn it off: when you add a company by link, its careers board is shared with the directory so everyone can find it. Your resume, roles and alert settings stay on your computer, and the app has no telemetry. It talks only to the job boards it scans, GitHub for the shared directory and for updates (once a day, and you can turn it off), the project's company inbox when you add a company (optional), and Telegram if you turn on alerts. Installing also downloads Node from nodejs.org.

How fresh are the jobs?

As fresh as your last scan. Scan whenever you like, or schedule 1 or 2 scans a day and get new matches on Telegram.

How do I run it?

Paste one line into a terminal (PowerShell on Windows, Terminal on macOS and Linux): it installs RawJobs, starts it when you log in and opens the dashboard, which walks you through setup. The lines are in Get it and the install guide. Developers can also run it from the source code with Node 22+ and pnpm.
