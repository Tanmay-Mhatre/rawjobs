    The problem

    Sent 50 applications and heard back from 2?

    Where you applied may be why.

    Job sites and agencies often repost jobs. Apply there and your resume can stop with the middleman. RawJobs links to the company's own careers page, so it lands in their hiring system.

    Where your resume goes

    Through middlemen 2 in between

    Through middlemen: your resume goes to a job aggregator, then a staffing agency, and often never reaches the company. No reply.

    Your resumeapplied on a job site
    Job aggregatorcopied listing
    Staffing agencytalent pool
    No replyThe company may never see it

    Direct to the company nothing in between

    Direct to the company: your resume goes to the company's own careers page and into its own hiring system.

    Your resumeapplied on the careers page
    Company careers pageLever, Greenhouse, Workday…
    Hiring teamGoes into the company's own hiring system

    Applying direct doesn't guarantee a reply. It removes the hops where resumes get lost.

    Get it

    Install it in about a minute

    Pick your computer and follow along. Nothing to download first, no account, no admin rights.

    You're on a phone. RawJobs runs on a Windows, Mac or Linux computer. Send yourself this page and open it there.

    Email me the link

    1. Press the Windows key, type PowerShell and press Enter. A window with a blinking cursor opens.

      Press ⌘ Space, type Terminal and press Return. A window with a blinking cursor opens.

      Press Ctrl Alt T, or open Terminal from your apps.

      The terminal is where you type commands. You only need it once.

    2. Click Copy. You don't need to understand the line.

      irm https://github.com/Tanmay-Mhatre/rawjobs/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex
      curl -fsSL https://github.com/Tanmay-Mhatre/rawjobs/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
      What does this line do?
      • Downloads RawJobs from GitHub and the Node it runs on from nodejs.org, and checks both against signed checksums before running anything.
      • Installs into your own user folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\RawJobs~/Library/Application Support/RawJobs~/.local/share/rawjobs. Nothing system-wide, no admin rights.
      • Starts RawJobs now and every time you log in, and opens it in your browser.
      • You can read it first: install.ps1 (opens in a new tab)install.sh (opens in a new tab).

    3. Click in the PowerShell window, press Ctrl V (or right-click), then press Enter.

      Click in the Terminal window, press ⌘ V, then press Return.

      Click in the Terminal window, press Ctrl Shift V, then press Enter.

    4. It downloads and checks RawJobs. You're done when you see RawJobs is running.

      Something else showed up? See Need help? below.

    5. Your browser opens RawJobs at 127.0.0.1:4317. It walks you through setup in about 3 minutes.

      Didn't open? Go to that address, or open RawJobs from the Start menu~/Applicationsyour app menu.

      That's it. You can close the terminal. RawJobs keeps running in the background and starts when you log in.

        Need help?Fixes for common problems, and how to uninstall
        
    
          
    
            
    “irm” isn't recognised
    You're in Command Prompt. Open PowerShell instead (step 1) and paste the line again.
    
            
    “curl” isn't found
    Run the line in Terminal, not another app.
    Install curl, or use wget -qO- https://github.com/Tanmay-Mhatre/rawjobs/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
    
            
    Your antivirus or work laptop blocks it
    Download the script from the latest release (opens in a new tab), read it, and run it from the file.
    
            
    The dashboard didn't open
    Open RawJobs from the Start menu~/Applicationsyour app menu. Still nothing? Run the install line again: it repairs RawJobs and keeps your data.
    
            
    Port 4317 is busy
    RawJobs uses the next free one (4318, 4319…), and the shortcut finds it for you.
    
            
    Uninstall
    Paste this into PowerShellTerminal. It asks whether to keep your data.
    
              
    & "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\RawJobs\bin\rawjobs.cmd" uninstall
    
              
    "$HOME/Library/Application Support/RawJobs/bin/rawjobs" uninstall
    
              
    ~/.local/share/rawjobs/bin/rawjobs uninstall

    FAQ
    
      
    Common questions
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Is it free?
    Yes. RawJobs is open source (MIT), with no paid tier and no account.
    
        
    Does it use AI?
    No. Scores come from rules you can read and change. An optional AI re-rank, with your own key, is on the roadmap.
    
        
    Which job sites does it read?
    Companies' own careers pages on __SYSTEMS_N__ hiring systems, plus __BOARDS_N__ public job boards: __BOARD_NAMES__. A shared directory, updated weekly, tells it where to look.
      __ATS__
    
        
    Is my data sent anywhere?
    No. Your resume and settings stay on your computer, and the app sends no usage data. One optional exception: when you add a company by its link, that careers page address is shared so everyone can find it. You can turn that off. Full details (opens in a new tab).
    
        
    Is the install line safe?
    It downloads RawJobs from GitHub and Node from nodejs.org, checks both against signed checksums before running anything, and installs only into your user folder. You can read the script first: Windows (opens in a new tab), Mac and Linux (opens in a new tab).
    
        
    How fresh are the jobs?
    As fresh as your last scan. Scan whenever you like, or schedule a scan every morning and get new matches on Telegram.
    
        
    How do I uninstall it?
    One line removes it and asks whether to keep your data. It's under Need help? in Get it.
    
        
    Can I add a company, or help build it?
    Yes. Paste a careers link in the app, or suggest a company (opens in a new tab) for the shared directory so everyone gets it. Each hiring system is one small file in the repo: see Contributing (opens in a new tab).
